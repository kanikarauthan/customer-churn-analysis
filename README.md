# 📊 Customer Churn Analysis Using SQL & Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn patterns for a telecommunications company using SQL and Power BI. The objective is to identify the key factors contributing to customer churn, understand high-risk customer segments, and provide actionable business recommendations to improve customer retention.

The analysis transforms raw customer data into meaningful business insights that can help the company make data-driven decisions.

## 🎯 Business Problem

The telecommunications company is experiencing customer churn, resulting in potential revenue loss and reduced customer retention.

The business wants to understand:

* Why are customers leaving the company?
* Which customer segments have the highest churn rates?
* What factors contribute most to customer churn?
* How can the company improve customer retention?

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **SQL (MySQL):** Data exploration, querying, and churn analysis.
* **Power BI:** Interactive dashboards and data visualization.
* **Microsoft Excel:** Data inspection and preliminary analysis.

## 📂 Project Workflow

1. **Data Preparation:** Examined the dataset for missing values, duplicates, and inconsistencies.
2. **Data Analysis Using SQL:** Queried customer data to identify churn patterns and analyze important business metrics.
3. **Exploratory Analysis:** Investigated churn across different customer segments.
4. **Dashboard Development:** Created interactive Power BI dashboards to visualize churn patterns and identify potential root causes.
5. **Business Recommendations:** Translated analytical findings into actionable customer retention strategies.

## 🔍 Key Analysis Performed

The project explores customer churn across the following dimensions:

* **Churn Rate Analysis:** Measured the proportion of customers who discontinued their services.
* **Contract Type Analysis:** Examined churn patterns across different contract types.
* **Tech Support Analysis:** Compared churn among customers with and without technical support.
* **Payment Method Analysis:** Identified payment methods associated with higher customer churn.
* **Monthly Charges Analysis:** Investigated the relationship between monthly charges and customer churn.
* **Customer Tenure Analysis:** Explored how customer retention varies with the length of the customer relationship.
* **Root Cause Analysis:** Investigated customer churn patterns to identify potential drivers of customer attrition.

## 📈 Key Insights

The analysis revealed several important patterns:

* **Overall Churn Rate: 26.58%** — Approximately 27 out of every 100 customers discontinued their services.
* **Contract Type:** Month-to-month customers represented the highest-churn contract segment.
* **Technical Support:** Customers without technical support experienced higher churn.
* **Payment Method:** Electronic check users had the highest churn among the payment methods analyzed.
* **Monthly Charges:** Customers who churned had higher average monthly charges than customers who stayed.

These findings highlight customer segments that may benefit from targeted retention initiatives.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following strategies could help improve customer retention:

### 1. Encourage Long-Term Contracts

Introduce incentives, loyalty benefits, or discounts to encourage month-to-month customers to switch to longer-term contracts.

### 2. Improve Customer Support

Strengthen technical support services, reduce response times, and proactively assist customers experiencing recurring technical issues.

### 3. Improve Customer Onboarding

Develop a structured onboarding process for new customers to help them understand available services, resolve initial issues, and recognize the value of their subscriptions.

### 4. Review Pricing Strategy

Evaluate pricing plans for customers with higher monthly charges and consider offering suitable packages based on customer needs and service usage.

### 5. Investigate Payment-Related Churn

Examine the customer experience associated with electronic check payments and explore whether payment convenience, billing issues, or other factors contribute to churn.

*Note: These recommendations are based on observed churn patterns. Further investigation would be required to establish the underlying causes of customer churn.*
